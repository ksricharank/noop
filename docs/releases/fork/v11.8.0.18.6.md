# 11.8.0.18.6 (build 407)

The second, smaller half of the scrolling problem. 18.4 removed the cause; this removes what was
left near the charts.

## What was still costing frames

`ScoreTrendSection.points` was a plain computed property that re-sorted the whole day-keyed series
and re-parsed every key into a `Date` on **every body evaluation**. Measured:

| Stored days | Per call |
|---|---|
| 58 (the maintainer's history today) | **1.2 ms** |
| 365 | **6.2 ms** |
| 1000 | **17.2 ms** |

Against a 16.7 ms frame budget — and the section appears on **both** the Sleep and Recap tabs, with
`comparison` and `valueRange` walking the same series beside it.

That is the worst possible shape for a cost: invisible on a fresh install, and steadily worse for
exactly the wearers with the most data.

## The memo's own hazard was worse than the cost it removed

Sleep passes `restByDay`; Recap passes `scoresByDay`. Two different series through one section.

A cache keyed on count and window alone would serve **one tab's points to the other tab's chart**
whenever the two series happened to be the same length — silently wrong data, which is strictly
worse than visibly slow data because nobody reports it. The key therefore carries an explicit
`cacheIdentity`, and deliberately **not** the display title: a `LocalizedStringKey` is not a usable
key, and keying on translated text would change identity with the app's language.

`ScoreTrendPointsCacheTests` pins that specifically — two equal-length series must not be
interchangeable.

## What was checked and deliberately left alone

`TrainingLoadChart` re-runs its min/max downsampling per body evaluation as well. It is
**byte-identical to upstream v11.8.0**, and upstream's own perf work from that release survived the
uplift intact: `AppModel` is out of `SleepView`'s root (#2295) and `BodyClockDialSection` is the
leaf that owns `circadianPhase`.

Left alone on purpose. Diverging from upstream to chase a cost it already knows about and has
already tuned is how a fork inherits a maintenance burden for nothing — and the measurement said
the fork-only path was the one that mattered.

## Verification

Both app targets build. 2435 tests, 2 failures — `SkinTempAbsoluteDisplayTests` and
`SleepCarriedStampTests`, both upstream's own files, both failing identically on a clean v11.8.0
checkout with no fork code present.
