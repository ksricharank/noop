# Fork release 10.6.0.14.11 (build 273)

The maintainer's 1 AM report against 10.6.0.14.10 ("there is some weird bug", 260831): after
midnight the Today header still said "Sunday, August 30", Key Metrics still showed Sunday's
accumulators (Strain 5%, 1632 kcal, 5,105 steps) while the synthesis strip counted Monday

commit on `feature/targets-widgets`; `feature/breathe-automation` rebased on top, stamp amended.

## The day rolls at local midnight on every fork surface

The maintainer's stated model, now the fork contract:

- **Numerators (steps / calories / effort) are the CURRENT calendar day's, everywhere** — app
  page, strip, widgets, banner, synthesis. At 00:00 they restart from zero (honestly "0/46",
  "0/2075" until the first sync banks the new day's row).
- **Targets (the denominators: effort/cal/steps targets + sleep need) may stay the PREVIOUS
  day's until the new night is scored** — they price off the carried anchor (last scored
  recovery), and that flips when the morning recovery lands: in practice the first strap sync
  after wake, i.e. min(wake, quiet-hours end) + one ~15-min burst. A night still in progress
  can't be scored, so sleeping past the quiet-hours end just means the flip waits for wake.

What upstream did instead: Today anchors on the LOGICAL day (rolls at 04:00, #16/#144 — "your day
ends when you sleep"), and `repo.today`'s anti-blank guard pins yesterday's row even past 04:00
until tonight's sleep banks. Meanwhile the fork's targets strip keys on max(logical, local) —
which rolls at midnight. The 00:00–04:00 window therefore showed two different days at once; a
night-owl maintainer lives in that window.

The change (`LiquidTodayView`, display-only — stored rows were always local-calendar-keyed):

- Offset 0 anchors on the local calendar day: header date, Key Metrics accumulators, and the
  since-midnight HR chart all roll at 00:00. `repo.today` itself is untouched (its carry
  semantics feed session math and the menu bar).
- Recovery-derived fields (Charge, HRV, RHR, respiratory, Rest) keep carrying via the existing
  prior-day paths (`lastVitalsDay` / `ChargeDisplay` / `widgetAnchor`), so the page never blanks
  and the targets stay stale-until-morning exactly as specified.
- The local day key joins the `load()` task id, so the rollover re-resolves on the next repaint
  instead of waiting for a sync. The day picker's upper bound follows.
- Classic (macOS) TodayView keeps the upstream 04:00 behavior — iOS liquid only.

## One effort numerator

`WidgetPublish` formatted the widget's effort side from the ANCHOR row's strain (upstream #313
behavior) while steps/cal already came from the targets' today row — the "5/46 vs 0/46" split.
Both effort sides now format from the targets (`effortTodayStored` / `effortTarget`), byte-same
as the Live Activity's `effortDisplays(targets:)` and the in-app strip; the 0–100 `effort` int
(upstream ring-face fill) follows the same today row so a ring and its centre text can never
describe different days. Scored-night fields (recovery/rest/HRV/resting HR) stay on the carried
anchor.
