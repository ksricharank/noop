## What the 260827-1840 log proved (the measurements behind this build)

- **The `.5` Health feature worked.** `wakes=14 synced=7 empty=4 avgSyncMs=3596` over ~4.2 h — from
  ~33 wakes/hour to ~3.3, empty share 80 % → 29 %, sync 3× faster. Read side intact
  (`apple-health=39` day rows, 7/7 sleep coverage). Case closed.
- **The `.5` re-score instrumentation named the culprit.** `compute` never exceeded 441 ms; the
  actor-queueing arm (`read ≫ sql`) never appeared. The same 84-query prep costs ~8 ms/query
  foreground and **~495 ms/query when the phone is locked** — iOS I/O-throttling the whole process.
  It is not the math, not the store actor, not contention: it is *where* the pass runs.
- **The dominant battery cost is the always-on realtime stream.** 1–2 BLE notifications/second,
  24/7, each a process resume — billed as 5–6 h/day of background activity. The log also showed the
  stream's stored-data value is concentrated: every R-R that landed came from historical offloads
  (`path=historical`), EXCEPT the dense night capture that HRV coverage (0.9 with the stream vs 0.4
  without) depends on. Daytime, the only locked-phone consumer is the Lock-Screen average.

## New: locked-stream duty cycle (`feature/locked-stream-duty-cycle`)

Stacked on `feature/lock-screen-hr-average`. Its Lock-Screen refresh setting gains a sentinel:
**-1** opts into a three-mode stream policy (any value ≥ 0 keeps today's behaviour byte-identical):

- **sleep window** (the #927 quiet-hours window, honoured regardless of the overnight-only toggle):
  stream armed — night R-R density and HRV coverage untouched;
- **unlocked:** stream armed — live surfaces stay live;
- **locked daytime:** stream silenced, except a 75 s spot burst every ~10 min (anchored on offload
  completions plus a best-effort timer) feeding the Lock-Screen average and a daytime R-R sample
  for stress/spot-HRV.

Mechanism notes, each load-bearing: the silence edge also **unsubscribes 0x2A37** (firmware can keep
emitting it with the toggle off — that survival is load-bearing for #927/#80, so the unsubscribe is
scoped to duty mode); the keep-alive's no-data bounce skips deliberately-silenced spans, with
liveness accountability moved to the burst; the policy only ever **narrows** the pre-existing want
(continuous-capture off, the strap-battery pause, and #927's window keep their authority); lock-edge
observers reconcile promptly (the keybag flips a beat after `WillBecomeUnavailable`, hence a +15 s
follow-up, with the ~60 s keep-alive as backstop). Settings field keyboard becomes
numbers-and-punctuation — the number pad has no minus key.

Pure policy in `Strand/App/LockedStreamPolicy.swift`, 9 pins (sentinel edges, the narrowing
contract, the three armed states, burst window edges, the clamp folding deeper negatives onto -1).

## Changed: locked background re-scores always defer (`feature/locked-rescore-deferral`)

The measured rule had a *recurring* hole: every fast foreground pass resets
`lastCompletedPassSeconds` to under a second, so the FIRST background trigger after each pocket-in
was waved through — into the throttled 56 s/66 s passes above, once per unlock/lock cycle, and the
wasted pass is what re-taught the rule. New pure-policy input: backgrounded **and locked** defers
whatever the measurement says. The debt coalesces later locked triggers into one settle (not a pass
per unlock); the sleep-window rule still outranks it (an in-window deferral must never schedule a
background task); unlocked-background keeps the measured behaviour exactly. 3 new pins.

## New: battery attribution in the log header (`feature/battery-instrumentation`)

Three header lines, silent-when-unused like the Health line:

```
BLE wakes: hr2A37=86400 puffin=1200 data=300 … — N total, N/h over N.Nh
CPU this app session: user=Ns sys=Ns
MetricKit (iOS's own daily bill, MM-dd): cpu=Ns fg=Nm bg=Nm
```

Per-characteristic notification counters (one integer increment per event — the instrumentation
cannot become the drain it measures), the process's `getrusage` CPU bill read once at export, and a
MetricKit subscriber parking iOS's own daily per-app numbers (collected by the OS regardless) for
the next export. This is the before/after meter for the duty cycle, and the tell for whether
firmware keeps 0x2A37 emitting with the realtime toggle off. 5 pins.
