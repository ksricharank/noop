# Fork release 10.6.0.14.7 (build 269)

Target-formula rework from the same-night review of 10.6.0.14.6, all on `feature/three-pillar-card`
(two commits); `release-10614-7` extends `release-10614-6`. The maintainer's verdicts, verbatim in
spirit: effort from readiness not history; a heart ceiling without an invented margin; a sleep need
that changes nightly with rest and charge, floored at 7 h and capped at 10 h.

## Effort target: the body's readiness, never history

The history percentile is gone. Today's charge picks the approved #43 recovery→optimal-strain band
(14–18 / 10–14 / 4–10 of 21); the multi-signal `ReadinessEngine` level (HRV/RHR/respiration vs
personal baselines + acute:chronic load and monotony) positions the target on a four-notch ladder —
rundown BELOW the band, strained at its floor, balanced at the midpoint, primed just under the top —
and last night's Rest score shifts one notch either way. The calorie target prices that effort
target through the personal kcal-per-effort fit, deliberately extrapolating past a sedentary
fortnight. Effort renders on the user's chosen display scale (their "optimal ≈ 10" is the 0–21
axis).

## Calm ceiling: the 85th percentile of your own daytime beats

The fixed +25 margin is gone. Every accepted daytime live beat (outside the sleep window) banks
into a persisted week-deep histogram (`DaytimeHrHistogram`, BatteryDiag idiom — one integer
increment per already-processed tick); the ceiling is the 85th percentile of the week's beats,
clamped 65–115. Cold start (< ~1 h of beats) and -1 duty-cycle daytimes fall back to RHR+25, and
the coach names whichever basis produced the number.

## Sleep need: composed from charge + rest, 7–10 h

base (p75 of your own nights, 7 h floor) + charge ask (recover +40 m / maintain +20 m) + last
night's Rest (<50 → +30 m, ≥85 → −15 m) + debt as the capped junior term (¼ ledger, max +45 m,
deadbanded), clamped 7–10 h. The v1 debt-cap constant that pinned the card at one number for weeks
is retired with a HISTORY note.

## Verify on phone

- Synthesis Activity bullet names the readiness-derived effort target (on the 0–21 scale) and the
  calorie decomposition; the card's Cal denominator leads the sedentary history.
- The HR denominator shifts from ~85 (fallback) to the histogram percentile after ~an hour of
  wear; the coach's ceiling line switches basis wording at the same moment.
- The Sleep column moves night to night with charge/rest instead of pinning.
