## Charge: a trusted partial pass refreshes today's night

The 43 stood for two hours because of the 260922 preserve rule — a partial pass (light, abandoned,
narrow) may fill a blank Charge but never overwrite one. Every pass from 06:21 to 07:41 computed the
finished night under `hrvNValid=21 trusted` and was told to keep 43; six full passes were abandoned
in the background before a foreground pass completed at 08:18.

That rule existed for partial passes scoring against a baseline folded only from the nights they
scanned (`hrvNValid=2`, the 31↔63↔30 flips). The 260922 baseline fix removed that cause in the same
build; the rule stayed as a belt. It now keys on the BASELINE instead of the pass KIND: under a
trusted baseline (≥ 14 valid nights) a partial pass refreshes TODAY's row as a unit — sleep, HRV,
resting HR and Charge together, so Charge can never sit beside a sleep figure it was not computed
from. Thin baselines keep the original rule exactly; earlier days stay numerators-only.
`DailyMetric.partialPassRefreshesNight`, pinned by PartialPassTrustedRefreshTests (3).

Expected effect: the morning Charge converges with the night as background offloads land (here,
~07:20 instead of 08:18 on open). Paired settings advice: set **Settings → Sleep ends** to the usual
wake time (it was 06:00 while the night ran to ~07:40), so the FIRST score comes from a finished night.
