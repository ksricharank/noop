## What 18.29's three hours on-device proved (log 260928-2110)

- `Store files: data=1736MB wal=1MB` — the WAL never exceeds 5 MB on any pass. **WAL-starvation
  theory: dead.** Checkpointing is healthy.
- The main database is **1.7 GB**, statements still run seconds each, and the longest pass hit
  **8360 s**. Paced sleeps hit their 30 s cap regularly (real, but secondary).
- Bycatch: `Re-score today: cpu bg=47051s fg=29032s` claims 21 hours of CPU by 21:10 — that tally
  is measuring something other than CPU (likely overlapping wall). Treat it as untrustworthy until
  it is fixed; the per-pass lines are the numbers that count.

## What this build measures (still no behavior change)

- **`Tables (≈rows):`** in the export's Daily-data block — per-table row estimates via max(rowid),
  never a scan, so the export cannot stall. This says whether the 1.7 GB is raw HR samples, R-R,
  motion, or something unexpected — and therefore whether the fix is retention, indexing, or a
  query shape.
- **`maxRead=`** on every pass's sql line — the single slowest read since the pass began. 500 s of
  read time as 100 × 5 s points at throttled I/O over a bloated table; as 3 × 160 s it points at a
  specific monster query.

## Verdict rules for the next log

3. Tables modest ⇒ the size is dead pages ⇒ VACUUM conversation.
