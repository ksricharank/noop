# 11.8.0.18.19 (build 420)

Five corrections from the first hour on 18.18, all on the screens.

## The sleep-debt card answers the question it raised

> right now should I sleep tonight for 8h 43? or 8h 43 + 1h 22?

Neither — and the card said "add about 1h 22m", which was the old rule. The footer now leads with
**Tonight**, computed by the one rule every surface uses (`DailyTargets.sleepNeedTonightMin`: need plus
a quarter of carried debt beyond the deadband, capped at 45 min), then **Need · Carried · Net** — four
short labels, because the previous four overflowed their columns. The read states the composition:
*"Sleep about 9h 04m tonight — your 8h 43m need plus 21m toward the 1h 22m carried debt."*

## "You broke the LLM connection" — no, but the card lied about why

> it looks like the LLM one works - it is just the error message when it times out that is confusing

`TabInsightCard` showed *"No summary available. Set up a coach provider in Settings"* for **any** nil
result — a timed-out or rate-limited request on a fully configured provider included. Nothing reached
the strap log either. Now: each tab hands the card its own last outcome, and the fallback reads
*"Couldn't write this summary — request timed out. Tap ↻ to try again."* The set-up hint appears only
when there is genuinely nothing to call. Every tab summary writes one `coach: <tab> summary — …` line
to the log, and a rate-limited request is retried once after 20 s — the four tabs regenerate together
on a new build, and a free tier answers the tail of that burst with 429s.

## One stages card

The "vs typ" delta now rides each stage row of the Stages card (the hypnogram with AWAKE · LIGHT ·
DEEP · REM), on both the Sleep tab and the Today host — green above the 30-day typical, amber below,
nothing on AWAKE (the model carries no typical for it). The standalone **Stages vs typical** card is
retired: it renders nowhere and is not offered in Arrange, whatever a stored layout says; its case
survives so old layouts still decode.

## Recap: one "Ask the Coach"

The Day-quality score card carried its own link beside the one under "What it means". Removed from
the score card; the link belongs with the summary it follows up on.

## Calendar: a legend per metric

Each metric's calendar now has its own range legend beneath it, ends labelled with the values the
palette's two ends stand for — the fixed 0–100 for Charge and Sleep, the published range for Day
quality, the window's observed min/max for everything else. For a lower-is-better metric (resting
HR, respiration) the green end is therefore the low value. The legend and the cell tooltips are
derived from one inverse of the colour scaling, so they cannot disagree.
