## New versioning scheme (starts with this build)

Fork builds now carry the fork's own **major.minor** on top of the upstream tag:
`10.6.0.14.1` = upstream `v10.6.0` · fork feature-set **14** · fix **1** within it. The fork major
moves when the `FEATURES` set changes; the minor moves for fixes/iterations inside the same set.
(Builds `.2`–`.14` predate the minor and burned a major per build.) Full rules in
`docs/FORK-RELEASE.md` § Versioning.

## The fix: locked card vanished ~15–25 min into every away span (iOS 26 staleness)

**Symptom (260828-0914, on `.14`):** be away from the locked phone ~15 min → the Lock-Screen card
AND the Dynamic Island disappear together; both reappear the instant the app is opened.

**What the log ruled out:** the `.13` fixes held — zero `Live Activity: start failed`, zero
`dropped a dead handle`; the two `holding the Lock-Screen average` lines show locked link drops
being held, not ended. Nothing ended the activity.

**The actual mechanism:** every locked repaint stamped the card with a cadence-sized `staleDate`
(~22 min) — the honest-greying safety net, designed for an OS that GREYS a stale Live Activity.
**iOS 26 instead REMOVES a stale activity from both surfaces.** During an away span the repaints
that would have renewed the stamp can stop entirely (the silenced link times out — held, correctly,
but no link means no syncs; and iOS killed the app process twice in the log's away spans, with no
BLE event to relaunch it while disconnected). The stamp expired; iOS took the card down. Opening
the app pushed one update to the still-existing activity, un-staled it, and both surfaces returned
instantly — the exact reported signature, and proof the card was hidden, never ended.

**The fix:** locked duty-cycle repaints carry **no staleDate at all** — the frozen window average
never claimed liveness, so it simply persists. Retirement is now event-driven instead of
clock-driven:

- a new **unlock-edge kick** (`protectedDataDidBecomeAvailable`) pushes once with the current link
  state — connected → the card refreshes a beat ahead of the resubscribed stream's own ticks;
  strap genuinely gone → a proper end (ends, unlike starts, are allowed from the background);
- the `.13` app-open kick stays as the second layer;
- live unlocked pushes keep their 120 s staleDate (they refresh every ~2 s; it still protects
  against a crashed app leaving a fake "live" number).

`LockedStreamPolicy.liveActivityStaleSeconds` is retired (HISTORY note left in place; its test pin
removed). Two rare-event log lines added: `locked repaint found nothing to adopt — a start needs
the next foreground` (the "dead until app open" signature, one per sync if it ever recurs) and
`locked repaint skipped — no HR rows in the averaging window`.

**Trade accepted:** if the strap is off-wrist for hours while the phone stays locked, the card
keeps showing the last average until the next unlock (which refreshes or ends it) — chosen
deliberately over the card vanishing.

Verified: both apps compiled; `LockedStreamPolicyTests` (8) + `DeviceLockStateTests` (6) pass.
On-device check: lock and stay away 30+ min (no touching) → card and island still present, showing
the last synced average → unlock → live within a second.
