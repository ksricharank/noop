## Calorie target: two counting fixes

The maintainer had never hit the calorie target in 60 days. The logs confirm it, for example 10-18
late evening: Effort 46/65 but Cal 2060/3030. Target = a full resting day (≈ 1,650 kcal here) +
the prescribed session priced through Keytel at its heart rate. Recent high-Charge sessions were
80–90 min at ~145–155 bpm, ≈ 1,300–1,400 kcal. Three causes; the maintainer chose to fix the first two:

1. **Gross vs net (fixed).** `DailyTargets.sessionKcal` priced the session GROSS, but
   `Calories.estimateDayEnergy` credits only `grossRate − restingRate` above the resting floor it
   already counts. The session's resting minutes were counted twice, a ~70–100 kcal gap no workout
   could close. It is now net.
2. **Worn seconds vs a full day (fixed).** The estimate credited resting burn only for seconds the
   strap saw, while the target prices 24 h, so every unworn hour (~70 kcal) was a permanent
   shortfall. `estimateDayEnergy(…, dayStartTs:)` now credits resting burn from local midnight to
   the day's last reading, worn or not. It is deterministic (no clock), the active energy is
   unchanged, and `AnalyticsEngine` passes the local day start. Stored daily calories move up on the
   next pass that scores each day: today on the next sync, the last 21 days on the next full pass.
3. **Walking earns no active calories (NOT changed, by choice).** Active energy is credited only
   above 50% HRR (~118 bpm here), the gate added against Keytel's low-HR overcount, while Effort
   counts from ~50% HRmax (~90 bpm). So walking moves Effort but not Calories, and the calorie
   target is still only closed by doing the prescribed session.

Fork-only: the Kotlin twins are unchanged, by the parity rule's fork exemption.

## Lock Screen targets widget: bigger numbers

The rectangular face loses its Steps / Effort / Cal / Water captions; VoiceOver keeps them. The
values go from 21 to 28pt. The long pairs (Steps, Cal, e.g. "10.4k/11.6k") get ~63% of the width
and the short ones (Effort, Water) the rest: at equal halves the long pairs scaled to about half
the short ones' size. Rows align on baselines. This was checked by rendering the 172×76 slot with
the widest real values (`10.4k/11.6k`, `1.15k/2.54k`, `6.2/13.5`): nothing overflows, and the
`minimumScaleFactor` floor absorbs what is left. The inline face and the Home-Screen faces are
unchanged.
