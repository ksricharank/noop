## Calorie target now prices the effort target (weight loss contract)

First use exposed the percentile target's flaw: p75 of a mostly-sedentary fortnight is reachable by
simply existing, so an effort-0 day read "close to target" by late evening. The target is now the
user's own **effort target converted to calories**: a least-squares fit over the last 14 paired
days (`kcal ≈ baseline + kcal-per-effort-point × effort`) gives the personal zero-effort baseline
burn and the personal cost of one effort point; target = baseline + slope × today's effort target.
At effort 0 the Cal gap on the card IS the exercise still owed. The fit refuses thin (<5 paired
days), flat, or inverted history — the charge-band percentile then stands in — and the coach
context carries the decomposition so the synthesis explains the number rather than presenting it.

## Synthesis: three bullets, explain before prescribe

The default synthesis prompt becomes three markdown bullets mirroring the card in its order —
**Heart**, **Activity**, **Rest & sleep** — each explaining what led to the numbers before
prescribing the active step toward the target, and pulling in other metrics only when strictly
relevant to that bullet's story.
