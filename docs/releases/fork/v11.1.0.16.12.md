**Build 323 is confirmed good on the device** ("the island looks right now") — the three-and-three
layout and the corner clearance both landed. This is the one refinement on top.

## The expanded view

    (        )      Sleep 8h05        Water 5/21
    Effort 3.2/10.7   Steps 4412/8000   Cal 605/2250

HR is **not** gone from the island. It remains the whole of the COLLAPSED presentation — the compact
trailing slot and the minimal slot — which is the one seen in passing. Expanding is the deliberate
act of asking for the day, and the day is these five.

The heart glyph goes with it. It was the not-connected cue FOR the HR value beside it (red = linked,
grey = dropped); with no HR in the region it would be an icon indicating nothing. It stays in the
collapsed slots, where it still has a value to qualify.

**Positions are unchanged, as instructed.** The half HR occupied is held by a `Color.clear` spacer
rather than collapsed away, so Sleep stays exactly where it is instead of sliding left into the
gap — the layout just approved stays put. The corner insets and equal-share centring carry forward
untouched, and `.center` remains deliberately unused (it sits under the sensor cutout).

## A test split that was overdue

The island tests read a single `islandBlock` covering both presentations, so "is HR in the island?"
had one answer. It now has two different correct ones — absent from expanded, present in collapsed —
and a test that cannot tell the two apart cannot pin either.

`expandedBlock` and `collapsedBlock` separate them, and the new test asserts BOTH halves. Asserting
only the removal would be satisfied by deleting HR from the island entirely, which is the opposite
of what was asked. That pairing is the same shape as the 16.10 lesson, where a correction about one
surface was applied to another.
