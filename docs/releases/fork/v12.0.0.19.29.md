## Calorie resting figure: verified, no change

The resting part of the calorie target (~1,700 kcal for this profile) is the revised
Harris–Benedict BMR (Roza & Shizgal 1984): male 88.362 + 13.397·kg + 4.799·cm − 5.677·age;
female 447.593 + 9.247·kg + 3.098·cm − 4.330·age; nonbinary or unset, their midpoint. It is
×24 h, from the profile's weight, height, age and sex, with defaults 70 kg / 170 cm / 30 when
unset. The coefficients in `Calories.male/female` match the published equation. Mifflin–St Jeor
typically reads ~5% lower. Either way, the target and the day estimate use the SAME resting
rate, so its accuracy does not decide whether the target is hit; only the session part does.
