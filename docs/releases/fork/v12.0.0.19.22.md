## Today stress bullet: what, why, and one matched action

The maintainer: the bullet "simply states what my stress level is … and how it compares to the
day". The ask was to explain why, and to add what to do about it in the context of what raised it.

On which scale, the maintainer asked before the build and got this answer: the **0–3 score**. It is
heart rate (plus HRV when that lens is on) against your own calm level, so it can name its driver.
It has a reading every 30 minutes, so it shows when stress rose. And it already leaves movement out.
The Baevsky index is one whole-day number of beat-timing regularity, with no timing and no driver,
and movement mixed in.

**Prompt (Now, bullet 2)** has three parts, in order:

- **WHAT:** calmer than my day, about the same, or more wound-up (a gap under ~0.3 counts as the same).
- **WHY:** the most likely driver, from when it rose or eased, the heart rate behind the latest
  reading against my calmest hour, whether I had been sitting still or had just exercised, and last
  night's sleep and Charge. It is named as a likely read, never a fact: a meeting, coffee or a meal
  look the same in the data.
- **WHAT TO DO:** ONE bolded action matched to the driver. A long still stretch gets a five-minute
  walk; a short night gets slow breathing rather than more coffee; no clear driver gets a two-minute
  breathing break. Calm gets no fix, a few words that it is working. Moving gets no action either.

The figures are unchanged: `(**X** now vs **Y** for the day, of 3)`. This bullet may run to ~45
words and is the only Now bullet allowed to reach for sleep or Charge. The 19.21 default
(`0x96f0677d3cc47f57`) is retired.

**Data (the STRESS block)** gains two lines, both taken from the existing curve, so there is no new
metric:

```
  Through the day (each reading is the hour up to that time, oldest first): 08:00 0.8 · 09:00 moving · 10:00 1.9 · 10:30 2.0
  Heart rate behind the latest reading: 83 bpm; my calmest scored hour today: 64 bpm.
```
