## "Is the Health app's 8% NOOP's doing?" — largely, yes

The 260928-1120 header: `Health sync: wakes=912 synced=15 … writeBack=107s×168` — against
`wakes=54 … ×4` two days earlier. The routine post-backfill write-back ran after every ~10-minute
strap offload, and each run deleted and re-saved a FULL 14-day window (sleep sessions with stages,
the 1-minute HR stream, workouts, nightly vitals). The Health app's background share is it
ingesting ~168 rewrites of two weeks of data by lunchtime. The 912 observer wakes are our own
echo — HealthKit observers fire on your own saves — but the anchored delta already excludes
NOOP-authored samples, so the wakes were cheap; the writes were the cost.

Fix, per the stats file's own decision table ("the WRITE-BACK dominates → write less often"): a
**30-minute spacing floor** between routine write-backs, applied at the two CALL SITES (the
post-offload hook and the BG refresh task) via `HealthWritebackThrottle.admit()` — deliberately
not inside `writeBackAfterNewData`, whose fork delta belongs to feature/health-read-only-sync;
the first attempt to edit it there conflicted this very release cut, exactly as the stacking
rules predict. Stood-down runs are counted and printed (`writeBack=…×N skipped=M`). The second
lever — narrowing the routine window from 14 days to the 2 an offload can change — is DEFERRED
to the health branch for the same reason; the floor alone removes the 40× frequency factor.
The foreground `sync()` path is untouched: an app open always exports.
Expected next log: writeBacks ~2/h with a matching `skipped=`, Health-app battery share down.
Also visible and still OPEN: the slow passes worsened (`longest 2272s (light-today)`, bg CPU
4642 s) — the standing unattributed issue, next in line.

## Synthesis: bullets, not a word wall

The default now mandates a fixed skeleton: a bolded twelve-word verdict, then 5-8 one-point
bullets (≤16 words each) under bolded lead-ins — **Now** (state, z-scored against own baseline),
**Next** (concrete timed moves), **Tonight** (wind-down keyed to tonight's target + habitual
midsleep, with one licensed "about HH:MM" derived bedtime — the sole exception to the
no-derived-numbers rule), **Watch** (a forming illness/strain signal). Quiet days stay short.
Reset the Today-synthesis override in Coach settings to pick it up.

## Titles: burn is not food

The remaining illegibility was semantic: "Grab a snack, 697/1476 cal" read a calorie-BURN gap as
a food deficit. The prompt now defines each metric's direction (Steps/Effort → move; Cal = energy
burned, never suggest eating; Water → drink), requires a complete actionable thought, and spells
out how to read n/target. Reset the notification-title override too, if one is stored.
