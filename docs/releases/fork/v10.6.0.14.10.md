# Fork release 10.6.0.14.10 (build 272)

The maintainer's same-day review of 10.6.0.14.9 ("I tried the app and I want the following
changes"), plus three mid-review additions (Steps n/t; burst-retrospective detection; steps NOT in
the island). Three commits on `feature/targets-widgets` + two on the new
`feature/breathe-automation` (stacked on it — the re-homing chain is now four deep).

## The targets row is Effort · Cal · Steps · Sleep — HR is gone from every targets surface

Both Lock-Screen widgets, the home widgets, the Live Activity banner and the Today synthesis strip
now carry:

- **Effort n/t** — today's effort over its target, on the user's display scale ("3.2/10.7").
  Numerator is `LiveTargets.effortTodayStored` (today's row, never the carried anchor day's).
- **Cal n/t, TOTAL calories** — the raw whole-day HR estimate over `DailyTargets.dayKcalTarget`
  = a full day of resting metabolism + the prescribed session through the Keytel model, rounded
  to 25. A REST day's target is honestly the resting day alone. (The 14.8 exercise-only framing
  is retired with HISTORY notes; the maintainer wants the mainstream-tracker total.)
- **Steps n/t** — the day row's calibrated count over `DailyTargets.stepsTarget`: charge-band base
  (recover 6k / maintain 8k / push 10k — population guideline numbers), readiness notches (rundown
  −2k, strained −1k; primed adds nothing — the session is where primed headroom goes), clamp
  4k–12k. Rendered as FULL counts ("3205/8000" — a "3.2k/8k" abbreviation was reversed on sight).
  Body-state, never the user's own step history.
- **Sleep** — unchanged ("8h05").

Surface map (the maintainer's exact slotting, four screenshot reviews deep): WIDGET faces (lock +
home) abbreviate Cal and Steps ("1.2k/2.1k", "3.2k/8k" — no room for two four-digit pairs); the
strip and banner keep full counts. The accessoryInline widget above the clock carries
"Cal 1.2k/2.1k · Steps 3.2k/8k" in bold (Effort/Sleep skipped by spec); the
rectangular below the clock is a 2×2 grid with no wordmark, 21pt bold values over 8pt labels — the
slot's ceiling (rows pair a long value with a short one: Steps · Effort / Cal · Sleep); the banner
runs .title3 bold with the icon shrunk to a cue; the in-app synthesis strip is the same 2×2
at 24pt with centred cells; the home widgets carry all four plus strap battery;
the banner carries all four in one row; the **Dynamic Island deliberately does NOT carry Steps**
("I don't need steps in the island") — it keeps Charge · Cal · Sleep with Effort in the leading
slot, today's effort alone in compact/minimal. One data colour per metric everywhere: Effort blue,
Cal amber, Steps green, Sleep cyan. Retired with the HR column: `burstAvgHr`, the breathe-cue LEVEL
read (red digits, `breatheEdge`) — HISTORY-noted; dead keys stay declared for decode compat.

## The HRV dip reaches the wrist + screen — including in the island-less daytime mode

`feature/breathe-automation`, two parts:

1. **Screen notification**: the existing auto-nudge (buzz + in-app card on a fresh, non-exercise
   HRV dip while still) gains a "Take a deep breath" notification (`BreatheNotifier`,
   IllnessNotifier idiom; permission asked at toggle time; fixed id so nudges replace). New
   "Screen notification" sub-toggle under Stress check-ins, default ON.
2. **Burst-retrospective detection** (maintainer: "yes, I want it"): with continuous HRV
   overnight-only there are no daytime live ticks, so each completed ~15-min offload replays its
   freshly banked R-R through the SAME detector (`StressOnsetDetector.replayOffloaded` — identical
   edge/sustain/rate-limit/exercise gates; quiet hours computed at each beat's own historical
   time; band-gate HR derived from the beats themselves). A dip still fresh at scan time (≤45 min)
   fires the buzz + card + notification, whose copy names the lateness ("detected on your last
   strap sync, about N min ago"). Watermark makes re-scans no-ops; the scan stands down whenever
   live R-R flowed in the last 5 min; a 2 h horizon keeps a first morning sync from replaying the
   night. Pinned by `StressRetroReplayTests` (fires once, replay-safe, exercise-gated, historical
   quiet hours).

## Synthesis

Scan-first shape (two "wall of text" reviews deep): each section = bold title on its own line, ONE
bolded ten-word takeaway line (the verdict), then 2-4 fragment sub-bullets ("Thing — fact"), every
cited number bolded, no prose paragraphs anywhere. Heart reads the
heart's TRAJECTORY (RHR/HRV vs personal baselines — the z-score lines are explained to the model —
drift, watchouts, overall prescription; the calm/STRESSED verdict left with the red digits).
Activity cites effort, total calories AND steps, each n/t, from the targets block. Rest & sleep
content unchanged.
