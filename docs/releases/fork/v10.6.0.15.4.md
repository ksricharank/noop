## Breathe sensitivity is configurable (target ~5 nudges/day)

The stress check-in card gains two knobs: **Dip depth** (fires when HRV drops below N% of
baseline; ships 60%, range 50–80) and **Dip must last** (ships 60 s, range 15–120). Raising dip
depth toward 70–75% is the lever for more frequent nudges. Calibration evidence: a new day-keyed
export line — `Breathe cues today: fired=N (live=L retro=R) last=HH:mm:ss` — so the next log says
what a setting actually yields. Engine defaults unchanged; existing detector vectors byte-identical.

## Target automations (A / B / E), all default-off toggles in Automations

- **Morning brief (A):** the day's plan as ONE notification the moment the first post-wake sync
  scores the night — "Charge 80 · 45 min workout @ ~139 bpm · 10000 steps · sleep 8h21 tonight" —
  held until a configurable earliest time (default 06:00). Fires before the app is ever opened
  (rides the background post-offload path).
- **Wind-down (B):** the EXISTING wind-down reminder (Alarms screen) gains "Use tonight's sleep
  target" — the reminder time then moves with the computed target (charge/Rest/body check/ledger)
  instead of assuming a fixed 8 h night. Re-applied after every fresh score; reschedules only on
  change.
- **Target pacing (E):** proactive catch-up nudges, not status updates: at two configurable
  check-in times (default 14:00 & 18:00), if the day is behind a configurable share (default 60%)
  of the prorated pace, one nudge says what would catch you up ("1500 of 10000 steps — about
  85 min of walking · your 45 min workout is still open"). At most one per checkpoint per day;
  on-pace check-ins are consumed silently. Existing automations (illness watch, inactivity
  reminder) untouched.
