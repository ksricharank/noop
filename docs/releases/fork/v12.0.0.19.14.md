## Sleep tab: the newest night no longer goes missing on a busy morning

Report: this morning the sleep score "would sometimes compute (as 93) and sometimes not (more often
not)". The log shows the scoring was fine: every pass from 06:08 scored and stored the 10-18 night
(460 → 542 min as the offloads landed, Charge 91). The tab showed the 10-17 night (418 min, 87 deep,
161 REM, score 85) under "Last night's sleep wasn't detected".

Cause: the tab browses a separate, fuller list of sleep blocks, loaded per `refreshSeq` and dropped
whenever the sequence moves during the load. The morning had ~15 re-scores (11 abandoned in the
background) plus syncs, so the sequence moved every few seconds and the list could stand on a read
from before the night existed. `repo.sleeps`, refreshed with the day rows, already had the night.

Fix: `SleepModel.navSessions` adds per-night entries newer than the fuller list's newest night.
The tab, the Today-hosted sleep card and the browse list all use it. New always-on rare-event line
`sleepTab: night lag — …` when the lists disagree or the night on screen is older than the newest
scored day.
