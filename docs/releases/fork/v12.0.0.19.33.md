## NSS widget: the chart fills the box

The maintainer on 19.32's widget: "lot of wasted space. use most of it for the chart. include baseline
as a different dashed line. indicate time of day at bottom. move the NSS score to top right".

- **Header:** one compact row. "NSS" sits on the left with a key under it (a dash, then "typical N").
  **NSS now is top right** at 30 pt in its band colour. The old "of 100" and the separate typical line
  are gone.
- **The chart takes the rest of the box,** about twice its old 46 pt height. The y-axis 0 · 33 · 67 · 100
  is kept, now with faint solid grid lines at all four.
- **Typical (the baseline):** its own dashed line, brighter than the grid and drawn over the curve.
- **Time of day under the chart:** first, middle and last reading, compact ("6:45a · 12:10p · 5:35p").

A mock of the layout was rendered at 170 × 170 (iPhone17,2's small widget) to check the fit.
