The maintainer's third same-day report (260831 11:10, on 14.12, continuous HRV now fully OFF —
background bursts only): "still not updating. Seems like something significant is off." The log
(`noop-strap-log-260831-1110.txt`) moved the fault UP a layer: the 14.12 repark fix held (no

missing evidence, and lets the next log decide.

## The ordering fix (the one behavior change)

`refreshAfterCompletedBackfill` ran the widget publish LAST — after the 260830 burst-retrospective
stress scan. Any hang, slow pass, or process suspension inside the scan silently starved every
widget publish while the syncs themselves kept landing (and iOS is killing/restoring this app many
times a day right now). The publish now runs BEFORE the scan: it is the user-visible surface; the
scan is a nudge that tolerates a sync of lateness by design. Whatever the scan turns out to cost,
the widgets have already been fed. (`feature/breathe-automation`, whose insert created the order.)

## The evidence (two new export header lines, both day-keyed + persisted)

- **`Widget publish today: full=<finished>/<begun> live=N reloads=N dedup=N last=HH:MM:SS
  steps=… cal=… effort=… sleep=…`** (`WidgetPublishStats`, `feature/targets-widgets`).
  Reading it: `begun > finished` = the publish path itself hangs or dies mid-run; healthy
  counts + a fresh glance under a stale widget = WidgetKit deferring repaints (the OS reload
  budget — we currently request a reload nearly every 10-min sync, ~100+/day, well above the
  typical background budget, so this is the prime suspect if the counters come back healthy);
  a stale `last=` = the publish is not being reached at all.
- **`Stress retro-scan today: runs=N lastMs=N maxMs=N`** (`RetroScanStats`,
  `feature/breathe-automation`) — the scan was an unmeasured every-sync background step.

Persisted (UserDefaults, reset on local-day change) rather than process-lifetime, because iOS
kills and state-restores the app many times a day and per-process counters would only describe
the last half hour. Both lines silent until the path ever runs. Pinned by WidgetPublishStatsTests
+ RetroScanStatsTests (9 tests).
