## High-NSS notification

"I want a notification when NSS exceeds 67." `NSSNotifier` runs on every `NormalizedStressStore.update`,
which follows the sync cadence. It reads the same smoothed reading every surface shows, and exercise is
never scored, so a workout cannot set it off. The gates (pure `decide`, pinned by `NSSNotifierTests`):

- **Fresh only:** a reading more than 15 min old neither fires nor re-arms.
- **Once per high stretch:** firing disarms it, and it re-arms only once NSS drops below 60.
- **At most hourly**, even across stretches.

The banner reads, for example, "Stress is high — NSS 72". The body has the card's why line, my typical,
the reading's age when 5+ min, and one line on slow breathing. **Tapping it opens the Stress tab.** It is
on by default; the switch, "Notify me when NSS reaches 67", is on the Stress tab's method card.
Permission is asked on the Stress tab. Posting writes `nss: high notification posted (NSS N, M min old)`
to the log.

## Today: a Stress (NSS) section

A new Today section, **Stress (NSS)**, sits under Heart Rate and is laid out like it: NSS now beside the
label, a status line ("Above Typical · 5-minute readings since 6:45 AM", or exercising / recovering),
the day's chart, and Min / Avg / Max. Tapping it opens the Stress screen. A saved custom Today order gets
the section back-filled at its default spot, before the first saved section that follows it in the
default order. It can be hidden or moved in Arrange.

## Axes on every NSS chart

"I can't see what values the chart is referring to." `NSSDayChart` wraps the Stress tab's own line
(`DaytimeLoadLine`, so the day is drawn one way) with:

- a y-axis at **0 · 33 · 67 · 100**: the calm / moderate / high band edges, where 67 is the notification
  line;
- faint grid lines;
- the first, middle and last times;
- a key: "Dashed line: your typical N. 67 and above is high."

It is used by the Stress tab timeline, the NSS card, Today's new section and the Today-hosted stress
card. The **square widget** gets the same 0 · 33 · 67 · 100 axis and faint grid at 33 and 67, rendered
at 170 × 170 to check that "100" fits on one line.

## The widget opens the Stress tab

`NSSWidget` has `widgetURL(noop://stress)`. `StrandiOSApp.onOpenURL` calls `NavRouter.openStress()`,
and the shell pushes the Stress screen onto **Today's** stack. That is where Today's chart opens it, so
back returns to Today. On macOS the sidebar selects Stress.

## Stress tab: "What your stress says"

"I want the top of the stress tab to have a more detailed explanation of just my stress situation
(another LLM prompt which should be added as a config to the AI coach)."

- **The card:** a `TabInsightCard` at the top of the Stress tab, open by default, with Ask the Coach.
- **The prompt:** a ninth editable prompt, **Stress summary instructions** (`ai.stressPrompt`), listed in
  the Coach screen after Sleep. It is in `promptCatalog`, so the "Make this my default" layer, absorption
  and the strap-log "Coach prompts" section cover it.
- **The facts:** the same NSS block the Today synthesis reads, the time now, last night's row, and the
  7 days before it.
- **The bullets:** right now (vs typical, in the block's words and gap; pace; drivers; exercise), today
  so far, likely why (as a read), what would help (one bolded action only at "well above my typical" or
  "unusual for me"), and this week.
- **When it re-asks:** on a 10-point step of NSS, a new unusual stretch, exercise starting or ending, or
  a new typical. Not on every sync.
