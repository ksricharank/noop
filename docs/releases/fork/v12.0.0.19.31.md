## NSS: exercise is not stress

The maintainer played pickleball and NSS "shot up to 89 which doesn't seem correct". The 261025-0911
log shows why. From about 07:00 to 08:15 the strap counted WALKING steps (pace `walk`) at 116–143 bpm.
The calm walk reference is 90, so zHR hit the ±5 clamp and NSS read up to 91. Pace comes from steps
alone, and running was the only exercise NSS skipped. Afterwards, sitting at 97 bpm read 70 (08:19–08:26),
and 90–96 bpm kept it at 42–47 until about 08:50. The auto workout detector kept nothing that day
(`kept=0`), and since #2187 detected bouts no longer become workout rows anyway.

`NormalizedStress.exerciseMarks` now marks two kinds of window:

- **Exercise:** the window's mean HR is at or above **50% of heart-rate reserve**. That is the day
  calorie model's exercise line; with resting 55–60 and max 179 it falls at about 117–120 bpm. Also
  exercise: running pace, or any overlap with a workout. A workout means any stored row (manual, Apple
  Health, imported, legacy detected, dismissed spans excluded) or **a workout running in the app now**,
  which has no row until it ends. A logged workout covers its whole span, even at a lower HR.
- **Recovery:** windows starting within **30 minutes** after the last exercise window, or after a
  workout's end. 30 rather than the 15 first proposed, because the log's post-game elevation lasted
  longer. Dips under the line mid-game (116–119 bpm) ride the same tail.

Both are left out of today's readings AND of the history the calm reference, typical and unusual
level learn from. The marks are derived at read time from the stored window features, so every stored
day re-scores on the next update without a stream re-read: this morning's 89 drops out of today's
average and "last unusual".

Where it shows:

- **Stress card:** "Exercise isn't scored" or "Recovering from exercise isn't scored for 30 minutes",
  each followed by "— this is your last reading."
- **Coach block:** the same for "now", plus "exercise today: N min, plus M min of recovery after it,
  left out of NSS".
- **`nss:` log line** gains `exercise≥118 bpm: N min exercise, M min recovery unscored (now: …)`.

Inputs come from `AppModel`: today's resting HR (default 60), the profile max HR, and the live
workout's start.
