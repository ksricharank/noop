# Fork release 10.6.0.15.6 (build 296)

Water becomes a first-class target, and every coach-written notification title routes through one
editable instruction. All on `feature/v15-widgets`, over the hydration feature already in the tree.

## Water in the synthesis area

- **`LiveTargets` carries the day's water** — the hydration tracker's own figures, read through a
  new SYNCHRONOUS cache (`hydrationTodayCachedML`, re-derived by every log/edit/delete and every
  sync) so the targets path stays pure, sync and memoized. One source for the Today row, the
  reminder copy, the derivation block and the coach context; they cannot disagree.
- **A third row spanning both columns**, under the 2×2 grid: `n/t cups` in the same big rounded
  type, plus **half-cup −/+ controls** — because water is the one target logged by hand, and water
  drunk outside a reminder still has to land. Both write through `logHydration`, the same call the
  hydration screen and the reminder action use. Minus retires the most recent entry, *shrinking* a
  larger one (a 500 ml bottle) rather than discarding it. The row is hidden when hydration
  tracking is off.
- **A WATER derivation block** in "How these were set", in the same plain-language shape as the
  other four: the baseline for your body, today's effort bump (with what a hard day *would* add
  when nothing is logged yet), the rounding, then cups at 237 ml each.
- **The synthesis Activity section now covers water** alongside effort, calories and steps, citing
  the same cups from the same block.

## Notification titles: one editable instruction, shared by every nudge

`AICoachEngine.notificationTitle(status:)` now serves the **pace check, the water reminder and the
move reminder**. The instruction behind it is editable in the Coach screen beside the Today
synthesis instructions ("Notification title instructions").

The 32-character bound is stated to the model **with worked examples** — which is what actually
produces short lines — and enforced in code, because only code can guarantee it: an over-long
reply is trimmed at a **word boundary** (never mid-word), and only a reply that cannot be brought
inside the bound falls back to the plain title. iOS clips a Lock-Screen title around 32 chars, so
a clipped title is the one outcome worth failing over.

Water reminders fire from repeating calendar triggers with no app running, so their title is
generated when the slots are re-armed (a cup logged, a sync, a settings edit) and cached for the
day — stated plainly rather than pretending the copy is live.

## Two answers worth recording

- **Reminders do NOT stop at the goal** (maintainer's call): they keep firing to the day's last
  slot, and the copy turns encouraging past target ("you're there, keep sipping") instead of
  showing a negative remainder. Hydration is not a finish line.
- **The pace check still says nothing when you're on pace.** The widgets already show that, and a
  nudge that fires when nothing is wanted is the fastest way to get notifications muted; the
  encouraging voice lives in the coach title of a real nudge.
