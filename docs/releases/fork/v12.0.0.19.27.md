## NSS re-cut: a calm baseline, a fixed scale, and "typical" / "unusual" beside it

The maintainer, on 19.26's "50 = your usual": "I want to make the assumption that over long periods
of time on average I am not stressed, and I only want to find the deviations where it is higher than
expected". They proposed a minimum-based baseline with 0 = the window's low, 100 = its high and
50 = its average, and asked whether there was a better alternative. Two parts of that were adopted
and one was not:

- **Calm baseline: adopted, as a low PERCENTILE rather than the minimum.** Per pace, the reference
  is the calm tenth: the 10th percentile of HR and ln SI, and the 90th of ln RMSSD. Even a mostly
  stressed window has a calm tenth. The minimum was rejected because the single lowest window is
  usually a glitch or a doze, and everything would read as stressed against it. Spreads stay robust
  SDs around the pace's median.
- **Scale: fixed, not stretched to the window.** NSS = 20 × combined z above calm, clamped 0–100.
  So 0 is calm, each 20 points is one typical swing, and 100 is five swings. A 0/50/100 scale
  stretched to the window was rejected: it would map a stressful stretch onto "50, normal" and a
  calm fortnight's wobble onto 100, hiding the deviations it was meant to find.
- **What is normal for me, shown BESIDE the number.** Typical is the median of the window's history
  scores. Unusual is their 95th percentile; an "unusual" episode is NSS ≥ that for ≥ 10 min.
  Both are nil until the history holds 100 scored windows (about a day).

**Where it shows:**

- **NSS card:** a pill reading UNUSUAL FOR YOU / ABOVE TYPICAL (+10) / TYPICAL / CALMER THAN TYPICAL,
  or NEAR / ABOVE / FAR ABOVE CALM before a typical exists. It shows "your typical N · today avg M",
  a why line against calm, and the last unusual stretch.
- **Timeline, the Today hosted card and the widget:** the dashed reference is now the typical
  (`DaytimeLoadLine.referenceLevel`, `WidgetSnapshot.nssTypical`).
- **Breathe card:** shown while an unusual stretch is still running.
- **Method card:** rewritten, with legend bands NEAR CALM / ABOVE CALM / FAR ABOVE.
- **Coach NSS block:** states the calm-based scale, "my normal over the last N days: typical T;
  unusual for me from U", band words against those, and HR "vs my calm" for each part.
- **Today prompt:** the NSS bullet's figures are `(NSS **X** now, **Y** my typical)`, or today's
  average while there is no typical. The one action comes only when the block says "above my
  typical" or "unusual for me". Retired fingerprint: `0xb6f193d799415c28`. New default:
  `0xd47d0517d761645`.
- **Trends prompt and NSS rows:** worded "0 is my calm state".

**Baseline window: free-form on the Stress tab.** "Baseline window ___ days" on the method card,
3–180, default **21** (the long pass's window). Apply re-reads now; a longer window backfills its
extra days 1–2 per update. Stored as `nss.baselineDays`.

**Migration.** Daily averages computed on the 19.23–19.26 median-centred scale are dropped once
(`Persisted.scale` = 2). The window features (pace, HR, ln SI, ln RMSSD) are unchanged and kept, so
no raw re-read is needed.
