# Fork release 10.6.0.14.8 (build 270)

The body-state doctrine release, from the maintainer's overnight review of 10.6.0.14.7: "every
target describes what is right for my body at this instant — not my history." One commit on
`feature/three-pillar-card`; `release-10614-8` extends `release-10614-7`.

## Activity: a prescribed session, priced by the app's own physiology

The 14.6/14.7 kcal-per-effort regression produced a "crazy" ~2,000 kcal target: its slope
extrapolated far past a sedentary fortnight, and the whole-day estimate it fed on credits resting
metabolism for every worn second (~1,400 kcal/evening of just existing). Replaced end to end:

- **The prescription**: charge band → base minutes (45/30/15), readiness notches it (rundown =
  REST day), last night's Rest shifts one notch; zone-2 @65% HRR, primed zone-3 @75%.
- **Effort target** = today's effort + exactly that session through the app's own strain curve
  (Edwards TRIMP inverted analytically). A from-zero 45-min zone-2 day lands at ≈10.7 of 21 — the
  maintainer's "optimal ≈ 10" intuition, recovered from first principles.
- **Calorie target** = the same session through the app's own Keytel model at the session's
  Karvonen HR (hundreds, not thousands). The Cal numerator becomes EXERCISE calories: the day
  estimate minus the resting accrual, so "0/450" in the morning means exactly "the session is owed".

## Heart: Karvonen, no margin, no window

Ceiling = last night's resting HR + 30% of today's heart-rate reserve (Tanaka HRmax), clamped
70–115. The week-deep daytime-beat histogram (shipped hours earlier in 14.7) is deleted — it was
still "what it has been the last few days".

## Sleep: population base + the body's day, 7–10 h

Tonight = 8 h population base (age-appropriate) + charge ask (+40 recover / +20 maintain) + last
night's Rest (+30 poor / −15 great) + readiness (+30 rundown / +15 strained / −15 primed) + the
capped junior debt term. The p75-of-my-nights base is gone by explicit instruction.

## Verify on phone

- Cal column reads like a workout tracker: ~0/target until you exercise; target in the hundreds.
- Synthesis Activity bullet narrates the session ("45 min at ~143 bpm") or says REST.
- HR denominator ≈ RHR + 30% HRR (for RHR 60, age ~35: ≈ 96–98).
- Sleep column moves with charge/rest/readiness; bounded 7–10 h.
