A same-day fix build: 10.6.0.7's duty cycle failed on first hardware contact, twice. Same eleven
branches as `.7`; the only content change is `feature/locked-stream-duty-cycle` rewritten (v2) and
one narrowed caption. The counter moves for log clarity — an `App: 10.6.0.7` header would be
ambiguous between the burst design and this one.

## What v1 got wrong (both bugs observed on the phone within hours)

1. **The locked Live Activity kept updating.** v1 silenced the SOURCES (TOGGLE 0 + 0x2A37
   unsubscribe) and trusted that no live ticks would arrive. But a 5/MG keeps pushing HR over the
   puffin data channels the offload machinery must stay subscribed to — so stray ticks kept reaching
   the Live Activity and repainting the locked Lock Screen. Silencing sources can never be airtight;
   there was no gate at the destination.
2. **The Dynamic Island sat stale after unlock.** The burst design armed/disarmed 0x2A37 repeatedly,
   and `setNotifyValue(false)` is asynchronous — a re-arm could read a stale `isNotifying == true`,
   skip the re-subscribe, and leave the subscription actually dead.

## v2 (this build): data-driven, no bursts

- **Sleep window:** full stream (night R-R density = HRV coverage, unchanged).
- **Unlocked:** full stream.
- **Locked daytime:** stream OFF. The Live Activity updates only when new data lands — once per
  completed offload — showing the **mean HR over the offload cadence's own window** (15 min
  normally, 60 in low-refresh mode, pinned against the cadence constants) plus the **last recorded
  recovery and effort** (the shared widget anchor).

Both bugs are fixed structurally, not patched: a pure `lockedLiveTickPushAllowed` gate at the Live
Activity itself makes a stray tick on ANY channel unable to repaint while locked; and with bursts
gone, 0x2A37 arm edges are rare (unlock / window open) and use a forced off→on cycle (the #613
re-establishment shape) instead of trusting `isNotifying`. Becoming active also reconciles, so
opening the app re-arms the stream instantly. Honest limit that remains: a SUSPENDED app cannot
learn of an unlock until its next BLE wake — but the locked card now shows averages-from-data, which
is correct while stale, and the next sync repaints it.

Plumbing: `AppModel.lockedActivityRefresh` (same shape as `healthWriteBack`, invoked beside it on
the offload-completion signal) → `repo.hrSamples` over the window →
`LiveActivityController.updateFromData`, pushing unconditionally with a staleDate covering one full
cadence plus slack.
