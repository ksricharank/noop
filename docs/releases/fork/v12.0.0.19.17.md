## The re-score policy (read this before touching any re-score trigger)

The authoritative write-up lives in the code, as the RE-SCORE POLICY block on
`RescoreBackgroundScheduler`. This is the summary.

**The three kinds of pass and what each may write**

| Pass | Window | Writes | Also |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quick (light) | today + yesterday | numerators only: steps, calories, effort | compares TODAY's night with the stored one; raises `nightMovedPending` when different |
| Full | 21 nights | everything: Charge, sleep, sleep blocks, baselines | completing one clears `nightMovedPending` |
| Interrupted full (`kind=abandoned`) | stopped partway | today's night only while it is still moving | leaves the re-score debt owed |

"Night moved" = `DailyMetric.nightInputsMoved`: sleep and stages, efficiency, HRV, resting HR,
respiration, skin temperature. **Charge is not an input.** It is a judgement of those figures against
a baseline, and a partial pass folds that baseline differently from a completed one (the 91 ↔ 92
afternoon). A missing stored row compares against a blank night.

**What a routine trigger runs** (a strap sync, the 30-min backstop):
1. The quick pass, first (not from midnight to the window's end, when nothing can move).
2. A full pass only if `routineFullPassWanted`: the quick pass saw today's night move, a re-score debt
   is owed, or we are inside the sleep window. Inside the window, the full pass's own policy defers to
   the window's end, which is what schedules the morning pass. Otherwise no full pass.

No timer anywhere. A failed or interrupted morning pass leaves the debt owed and the flag raised, so
the next sync retries. Late night data after sleeping in, the phone away from the strap overnight, or
a nap all show up as "night moved" and get their full pass on that sync.

**Not routine, never gated:** edits, imports, recalibrations, heals, the Sleep tab and Today
refreshes, and the owed-debt resume on foreground / background-processing time.

**Also:** quick passes no longer write sleep blocks, motion or band series, or run the duplicate
sweep. The 10-17 clipped copy behind the Sleep-tab "wasn't detected" morning first got in through a
22:43 quick pass.
