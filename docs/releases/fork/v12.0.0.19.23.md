## Why

The maintainer found the Stress tab's stress "very flat through the day" and wanted stress measured
while moving too, "deviations over and beyond hr change because of movement". The existing scores
cannot do either:

- The **daily 0–3 score** (top gauge, Markers tile) is last night's resting HR and HRV against the
  30-day mean. It is one number a day.
- The **daytime 0–3 timeline** scores each hour against TODAY's own calm hours and masks every hour
  that is ≥ 30% ambulatory. So it is flat by construction and blind while moving. The
  personal-baseline lens in the code has no switch in the app.

## The method (the maintainer's spec, documented on `NormalizedStress`)

The approach is ambulatory "additional heart rate": activity explains most HR/HRV change, and the
residual is the stress-like part.

1. **Personal model per pace.** Paces are still, pottering, walk, brisk walk, and moving without
   steps; running is not scored. The model uses 14 days of waking-hour (06–22) 5-minute windows. For
   each pace it takes the medians of HR, ln Baevsky SI and ln RMSSD, and their robust spreads
   (1.4826 × MAD). Pace comes from strap locomotion ticks per minute, plus wrist motion for step-less
   movement. A pace needs an hour of history before it is scored.
2. **Residuals** over a rolling 5-minute window, as z's: HR above, SI above, RMSSD below the pace's
   median. Each z is clamped to ±4.
3. **NSS = 50 + 15 × (0.40·zHR + 0.35·zSI + 0.25·zRMSSD)**, clamped to 0–100 and smoothed with an
   EMA (α 0.2 per minute, reset after a 10-minute gap). A missing HRV term (too few clean beats) is
   dropped and the weights renormalised, so HR alone still scores. 50 means usual for the pace; each
   15 points is one robust SD. The Stress screen's bands apply at NSS × 3/100, so ≥ 67 is HIGH.
   A high episode is NSS ≥ 67 for at least 10 minutes.

**Honest limits** (also shown on the card): it measures excess sympathetic drive. Heat, caffeine,
digestion, sleep debt, illness and talking raise it too. It saturates near max effort, which is why
running is unscored. It uses time-domain measures only. Daytime wrist R-R is noisy; if the score proves
jumpy, the HRV weights are the dial. It is **unvalidated**.

## Where it shows

- **Cadence:** `NormalizedStressStore.update` runs after every post-offload quick pass, inside the
  same background assertion. Foreground surfaces call `updateIfStale` (4 min). Today's windows are
  1-minute steps appended incrementally, with the last 5 minutes recomputed. History days are
  computed once each and kept in `nss-v1.json` (window features only, never raw). A fresh install
  backfills 1 day per background update and 2 per foreground one.
- **Stress tab:** a new NSS card under the hero. It shows NSS now /100 with a band pill, today's
  average, a one-line why ("While walking: heart rate 9 bpm above your usual for this pace, HRV lower
  than usual"), today's line, the last high, and the honesty caption. Until a pace is learnt it reads
  "Learning … N of 14 days".
- **Today:** the "Stress" card's value is NSS now, replacing the 0–3 score, captioned "NSS · stress
  beyond activity".
- **Coach:** a new NSS block. It gives now (band, pace, age when 15+ min), the why in words (heart rate
  vs usual at this pace in bpm; HRV and beat pattern vs usual), today's average with a reading every
  30 min, the last high, and running / still-learning states.
- **Today prompt, Now = three bullets again:**
  1. **NSS:** now vs today's average; why, from its parts plus the likely cause (as a read); ONE
     bolded action only above 60. Figures: `(NSS **X** now, **Y** today's average)`.
  2. **Heart:** heart rate vs resting, its direction, and why.
  3. Movement, activity and hydration (unchanged).

  Retired default fingerprints: `0xabc7e8a2a56f2bad` (the 261023 "am I stressed" default) and the
  261022 steps before it. The new default is `0xb6f193d799415c28`.

## Validation: what the logs must show

Each update logs a line like this:

`nss: now 64 (raw 70, walk, 2 min old, hr 106 vs 97 usual, zHR 1.8 zSI 0.9 zRMSSD 1.2) · day 52 over 412 min · model 14d/2688 windows (still,light,walk) · today +12 window(s) · backfilled 1 day(s) · 140 ms`

- **First days:** `backfilled` counts down, and `model` gains days and paces. The cost (`ms`) stays
  small after backfill.
- **Over several days, against moments the maintainer notes** (a stressful meeting, a calm evening,
  a breathing break): NSS rises and falls with them. A usual walk should read near 50, not high.
  One match is not validation; it has to track several.
- Watch `zSI` and `zRMSSD`. If they swing ±4 on ordinary windows, the HRV weights need lowering.
