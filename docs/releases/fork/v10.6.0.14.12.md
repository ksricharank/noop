## The two stranding holes in the #1539 escape (both fixed)

Upstream #1539 already parks a timeout-free `central.connect` while the pause is latched, so a
freed/recovered strap self-claims without the user. Two holes kept it from ever arming:

1. **Ordering**: the trip sites call `standingConnectWhilePausedIfDue(justTripped: true)` while
   `state.connected` is still true (inside `didDisconnectPeripheral` before the flag clears; on a
   live link in the refusal path), so the gate's `!connected` check declined SILENTLY — the log
   has no "parking a standing connect" line.
2. **Floored decline = stranding**: the re-park on the paused disconnect/fail paths is floored
   (one per 10 min), but a floored decline schedules nothing — and with no park outstanding no
   BLE event can ever wake a backgrounded app to try again. "One attempt per window" had no next
   window.

Fix: a per-episode cycle budget (`bondLoopParkCycles`, cap 30 ≈ 5–10 min of ~10–20 s cycles).
Within it the paused disconnect and `didFailToConnect` paths re-park IMMEDIATELY (floor
bypassed), so a parked connect is always outstanding and a transient blip self-heals the moment
the strap is reachable and bondable; cycle 1 (the trip's own disconnect) arms the initial park.
Past the budget the floored one-per-window behaviour returns, so a strap genuinely held by the
WHOOP app costs minutes of bounded retrying per pause episode, not a night. The budget resets
wherever the pause resets (user Connect, teardown, device removal, genuine bond). Pinned by two
new BondLoopHardeningTests (budget edge + cap size); suite green (14 tests).
