## 1. The high-NSS alert buzzes the strap

"when my NSS exceeds the target, I don't feel a vibration on my whoop."

- **Cause:** `NSSNotifier` (19.32) only posted a phone notification. Nothing ever asked the strap to buzz.
- **Fix:** `NormalizedStressStore.onHighAlert`, set by `AppModel`, runs right after the notification
  posts. It uses `buzzStrapOnce()`, the acknowledged sequence the other nudges and the Live
  "Buzz strap" button use (pattern 2 + RUN_ALARM on a 5/MG).
- **Switch:** new `BuzzCue.nssHigh` (`auto.wristBuzz.nssHigh`), default on and, like the stress
  check-in, independent of the Automations "Buzz my wrist" master. Shown as "Buzz my strap too" on the
  Stress tab's Alerts card and as "High stress alerts" under Automations › Buzz my wrist (same key).
- **Log** (always on, rare event): after each `nss: high notification posted …` line, one of
  `nss: strap buzz sent`, `nss: strap buzz off (Automations)`, or
  `nss: strap buzz skipped — strap not bonded`.
- Test: `NudgeWristBuzzTests.testTheHighStressAlertBuzzesWithTheMasterOff`.
