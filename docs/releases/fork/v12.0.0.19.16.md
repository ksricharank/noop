## Charge 91 ↔ 92, the quick pass, and fewer full passes

Report: Charge "moving subtly up and down — between 92 to 91". The night stopped changing at 07:40
(542 min, HRV 46.3), yet completed full passes wrote 91 every time (×18) while quick and interrupted
passes wrote 92 (×58). A partial pass folds its baseline from stored history; a completed one folds
it from the nights it just scored. On identical inputs the two land a point apart, and the last pass
to finish won the screen.

Maintainer's rules:
- **Quick (light) pass = numerators only.** Steps, calories, effort. It never writes Charge or
  sleep: no trusted refresh, no blank fill. Over a missing row it writes the numerators and no
  night.
- **Charge and sleep come from full passes.** An interrupted full pass refreshes today's night only
  while the night itself is still moving (the morning offloads: sleep/stages, efficiency, HRV, RHR,
  respiration, skin temp — `DailyMetric.nightInputsMoved`). Once the night settles, the completed
  pass's Charge stands.
- **Fewer full passes.** Every sync with new heart-rate data (all day long) started a 21-night
  re-score. Routine triggers (post-offload, the 30-min backstop) now run one only inside the sleep
  window, for 3 h after it ends (the morning settle), or when the last completed full pass is ≥ 3 h
  old. The light pass moves the numerators in between. Edits, imports, recalibrations, heals, the
  Sleep tab refresh and the owed-debt resume are not spaced.
