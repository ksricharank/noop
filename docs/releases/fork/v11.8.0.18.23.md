## The fixes (independent, each sufficient)

- **`reRunWindowDays`**: the re-arm floors its window at the standard 21 days. A wide one-shot
  (recalibrate-all) still re-runs at its own width; a light pass's 2-day window can never shape a
  full re-run again.
- **`isPartialBaseline`**: `partialBaseline = light || abandoned || maxDays < 21`. Any pass
  narrower than the standard window — whatever its kind, whoever calls it — now seeds its baseline
  from stored history and merges over stored rows (fill, never overwrite). Such a pass logs
  `kind=narrow`, so a recurrence is attributable on sight.
